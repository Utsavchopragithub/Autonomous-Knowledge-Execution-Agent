Company Employee Handbook — Excerpt (Unstructured)

Welcome to the company. This section covers general wellbeing policies in
plain language rather than a strict rulebook, since exact numbers are
sometimes still being finalized by HR for the current fiscal year.

On time off: employees typically get around 20 days of annual leave, though
the exact figure depends on tenure and department, and HR has mentioned this
may be revised downward slightly this year pending finance approval. Sick
days are handled more informally and are usually not deducted from the
annual leave count as long as they are reasonable in frequency.

On equipment: if your laptop or workstation is giving you trouble, the
fastest way to get help is usually to just walk over to the IT desk on the
3rd floor, though during busy periods you may be asked to file a ticket
instead so the request doesn't get lost.

On remote work: the company has a hybrid-friendly culture. Most teams allow
some flexibility for working from home, but this varies by manager and is
not strictly capped at a fixed number of days across the whole company --
some teams are stricter than others.

On security: never share your password with anyone, including IT staff.
Any password reset must go through proper verification. Account changes of
a sensitive nature are always double-checked by a human before being
finalized, regardless of who is asking.
